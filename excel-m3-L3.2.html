LESSON 3.2

Creating Bar, Line, & Pie Charts for Data Visualization

Lesson Overview & Key Takeaways

In this lesson, you will learn how to turn raw numbers into clear, visual charts and graphs:

  • Bar & Column Charts: Ideal for comparing different categories or items side-by-side.
  • Line Charts: Best for displaying trends and changes over time (months, quarters, years).
  • Pie Charts: Perfect for showing proportions and percentages of a whole dataset.
