Formatting an Official Corporate Memorandum

Apply what you have learned in Module 2 by formatting the raw memorandum text and schedule data provided below into a professional, standardized corporate document.

📋 Challenge Requirements:

  1. Memo Header Layout – Properly structure the MEMORANDUM header (TO, FROM, DATE, SUBJECT) with clean alignment and a dividing line.
  2. Insert and Format Tables – Organize the meeting schedule or task distribution list into a clean table with appropriate borders, cell padding, and header shading.
  3. Adjust Page Margins & Spacing – Set standard margins (1 inch) and maintain professional line spacing (1.15 or 1.5).
  4. Apply Heading Hierarchy – Use proper font styles and sizes (e.g., Bold, professional fonts like Arial or Calibri) for section headings.
  5. Final Polish – Ensure proper alignment, professional tone, and clean document flow.

📄 Raw Data to Organize & Format:

Memo Header Info

TO: All Administrative Division Personnel
FROM: Office of the Director
DATE: October 29, 2026
SUBJECT: Implementation of Standardized Digital Workflow and Quarterly Review

1. Purpose

This memorandum serves to formally announce the rollout of our new digital workflow templates across all departments to reduce document processing bottlenecks and streamline daily operations.

2. Implementation Schedule (Table Format)

Date | Department | Activity / Focus Area

Nov 03, 2026 | Finance Division | Word Document Standardization

Nov 10, 2026 | Human Resources | Excel Data Tracking & Formulas

Nov 17, 2026 | Operations Team | PowerPoint Presentation Pitch

3. Action Required

All department heads are required to attend the orientation session and submit their feedback reports on or before November 25, 2026. Your cooperation is highly appreciated.

*Note: Copy this raw memo text into Microsoft Word and format it professionally following the instructions above.
