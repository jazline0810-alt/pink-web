LESSON 1.1

Navigating Slides, Layouts, & Presentation Views

Lesson Overview & Key Takeaways

Welcome to PowerPoint Design! In this lesson, you will master the basics of presentation structure:

  • Slides: The individual pages that make up your visual presentation deck.
  • Layouts: Pre-arranged placeholders for titles, text boxes, and multimedia content.
  • Presentation Views: Switching between Normal, Slide Sorter, and Reading views.
