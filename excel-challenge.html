Building a Monthly Sales Report

Apply essential Excel formulas and functions from Module 2 (including SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, MAX, and MIN) by organizing and computing the raw sales data provided below into a professional spreadsheet.

📋 Challenge Requirements:

  1. Data Entry – Input the raw sales data provided below into an Excel spreadsheet with proper column headers.
  2. Basic Math Operations – Compute the total sales per item (Quantity Sold × Unit Price) using cell references and multiplication formulas.
  3. Apply Excel Functions – Use SUM for total revenues, AVERAGE for mean sales, and MAX/MIN to find peak and lowest sales.
  4. Formatting & Polish – Apply currency formatting (₱), proper cell borders, background header shading, and clear alignment.

📊 Raw Data to Organize & Compute:

Spreadsheet Content / Table Data:

Item Code | Product Description | Quantity Sold | Unit Price (PHP)

PRD-001 | Office Executive Chair | 45 | 3,500.00

PRD-002 | Ergonomic Mesh Desk | 30 | 6,200.00

PRD-003 | Wireless Mechanical Keyboard | 120 | 1,800.00

PRD-004 | Dual Monitor Stand | 75 | 2,400.00

PRD-005 | LED Desk Lamp with USB Port | 90 | 950.00

PRD-006 | Document Filing Cabinet | 25 | 4,500.00

PRD-007 | Professional Shredder | 15 | 5,100.00

Required Summary Metrics to Compute via Formulas:

  • Total Revenue generated across all products (SUM)
  • Average Quantity Sold and Average Unit Price (AVERAGE)
  • Highest and Lowest Total Item Sales (MAX and MIN)
  • Total number of unique product entries (COUNTA)

*Note: Input this raw data into Excel, compute the total per row using formulas, and add the required summary metrics.
