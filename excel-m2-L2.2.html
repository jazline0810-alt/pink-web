LESSON 2.2

Working with SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, & MAX/MIN

Lesson Overview & Key Takeaways

In this lesson, you will master the five most used statistical functions in Excel:

  • SUM & AVERAGE: Rapidly adding up values and calculating averages across data ranges.
  • COUNT & COUNTA: Counting numerical inputs vs counting non-empty cells.
  • MAX & MIN: Identifying highest and lowest values instantly in large datasets.
