LESSON 3.2

Adding Citations, Captions & References

Lesson Overview & Key Takeaways

In this lesson, you will master academic and professional referencing tools in Microsoft Word:

  • Citations & Bibliography: Adding sources and automatically generating reference lists in APA, MLA, or Chicago formats.
  • Figure Captions: Labeling images, tables, and figures systematically so they can be tracked easily.
  • Table of Figures: Creating a dedicated index list for all illustrations and tables in your document.
