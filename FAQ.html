Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

1. Is GrindMind free to use?

Yes! All our basic Microsoft Office tutorials, modules, and interactive materials are completely free for students and learners.

2. Do I need to create an account to start learning?

You can browse our modules freely, but creating an account allows you to track your progress, take quizzes, and save your learning journey.

3. What applications are covered in the courses?

Currently, we cover the essentials: Microsoft Word, Microsoft Excel, and Microsoft PowerPoint, tailored from beginner to intermediate levels.

4. Can I access GrindMind on my mobile phone?

Yes, our platform is designed to be responsive and accessible across different devices, including smartphones and tablets.
