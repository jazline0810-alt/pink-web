LESSON 2.1

Color Themes, Typography, & Visual Hierarchy

Lesson Overview & Key Takeaways

In this lesson, you will learn how to design clean and professional slides using core visual principles:

  • Color Themes: Choosing harmonious color palettes and applying them effectively across slides.
  • Typography: Selecting readable fonts, managing sizes, and structuring text for maximum clarity.
  • Visual Hierarchy: Guiding the audience's attention by arranging elements based on importance.
