LESSON 3.1

Sorting, Filtering, & Conditional Formatting

Lesson Overview & Key Takeaways

Welcome to Module 3! In this lesson, you will master techniques to organize and visually highlight key data points:

  • Data Sorting: Ordering rows alphabetically (A-Z) or numerically (High to Low / Low to High).
  • Auto-Filters: Displaying only specific rows that meet selected criteria using dropdown filters.
  • Conditional Formatting: Highlight top 10% values, duplicate entries, or highlight cells based on thresholds using color scales.
