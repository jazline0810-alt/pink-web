LESSON 2.2

Working with Icons, SmartArt, & Data Graphics

Lesson Overview & Key Takeaways

In this lesson, you will learn how to bring your slides to life by adding and organizing visual components:

  • Vector Icons: Replace text bullet points with clean vector icons to improve visual retention.
  • SmartArt Diagrams: Convert bulleted lists into process flows, hierachy trees, or cycle diagrams in two clicks.
  • Data Graphics: Simplify charts by removing gridline noise and using accent color on key data points.
