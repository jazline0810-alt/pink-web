LESSON 2.2

Page Margins, Orientation & Page Breaks

Lesson Overview & Key Takeaways

In this lesson, you will learn how to customize document structure and control page flow properly:

  • Page Margins : Adjusting spacing around the edges of your document for standard printing and readability.
  • Orientation: Switching smoothly between Portrait (vertical) and Landscape (horozontal) layouts.
  • Page Breaks: Manually pusing content to a new page without spamming the Enter key.
