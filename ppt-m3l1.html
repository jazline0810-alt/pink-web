LESSON 3.1

Applying Smooth Transitions & Subtle Animations

Lesson Overview & Key Takeaways

Welcome to PowerPoint Design! In this lesson, you will master the basics of presentation structure:

  • Morph Transition: Use Morph to create seamless object animations across slides effortlessly.
  • Entrance Effects: apply subtle fade or wipe animations to reveal points one by one.
  • Timing Control: Keep animations short (under 0.5 seconds ) to maintain a fast, professional presentation pace.
