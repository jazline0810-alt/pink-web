LESSON 1.2

Adding Text, Shapes, Images, & Formatting Elements

Lesson Overview & Key Takeaways

In this lesson, you will learn how to bring your slides to life by adding and organizing visual components:

  • Text & Typography: Formatting titles and bullet points for maximum readability.
  • Shapes & Icons: Using built-in vector shapes to highlight important ideas.
  • Images & Alignment: Inserting pictures and utilizing alignment tools to balance your design.
